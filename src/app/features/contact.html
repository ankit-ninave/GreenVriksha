Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know where Sunday's drive is?

Drive locations are announced every Saturday evening in our WhatsApp group. Join the group to get notified.

Can children participate?

Yes! Children aged 10+ are welcome, accompanied by a parent or guardian. It's a great family activity.

Do I need to register every Sunday?

Once you fill the volunteer form, you're part of the community. No registration needed for each drive — just show up!

What if I can only come occasionally?

That's perfectly fine. Come whenever you can — every Sunday and every volunteer counts equally.

Can I organize a drive for my colony?

Absolutely. Fill the Plantation Request Form and our team will plan a dedicated drive for your area.

How do I donate saplings or seeds?

Fill the Donation Form and our team will coordinate pickup or drop-off within 24 hours.
