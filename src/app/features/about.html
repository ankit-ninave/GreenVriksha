Our Story

Building a Greener Nagpur, One Tree at a Time

GreenVriksha Nagpur is a grassroots community initiative dedicated to making Nagpur greener through weekly tree plantation drives, seed collection, and environmental awareness.

वृक्षवल्ली आम्हा सोयरी वनचरे — संत तुकाराम

GreenVriksha Nagpur was founded with a simple but powerful belief — that every Nagpurian has the power to make their city greener. We started as a small group of friends planting trees on Sunday mornings, and today we're a growing community of 500+ volunteers spanning urban and rural Nagpur.

Our mission is to create a sustained, community-driven green cover across the Nagpur district by planting native trees, collecting and distributing seeds, running cleanliness drives, and ensuring every planted tree survives through follow-up care.
