Before You Request

  • Permission — Do you have permission (or can arrange it) from the land owner / authority?
  • Water Availability — Is water available nearby for post-plantation care?
  • Community Interest — Are local residents willing to help care for the trees after planting?
  • Site Access — Can our team access the site safely on a Sunday morning?
💡

Don't worry if you don't have all of these sorted. Fill the form and we'll work with you to figure out the details. We evaluate every request with care.
